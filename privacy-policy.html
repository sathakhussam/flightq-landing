2. Our role and yours

Under the DPDP Act, the organisation that decides why and how personal data is processed is the Data Fiduciary. An organisation that processes data on its behalf is a Data Processor. Our role depends on where your data comes from.

  • On our Sites, and for our own customer relationships (for example when you request a briefing or write to us), we are the Data Fiduciary.
  • Inside a Product, the records are owned and controlled by the organisation that subscribes to it: usually your airline, academy or FTO (the "Customer"). The Customer is the Data Fiduciary and we are its Data Processor. We process that data only on the Customer's instructions and under our contract with them.

If you are a cadet, instructor or staff member using a Product through your organisation, your organisation's privacy notice also applies, and requests about your records should usually go to them first. We will help them respond, and you can always contact us (see section 12).

3. Data we collect

When you visit our Sites or contact us

  • Contact and enquiry details: your name, work email, organisation, role and anything you write in a briefing request or email.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and the time of your visit, collected through server logs and essential cookies.

When you use a Product

  • Account details: name, email address, role, organisation and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile name and photo. We request only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile) and never access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.
  • Training records: enrollment details, batch, ground school and exam results, flight hours and logbook entries (including records synced from your FTO's software), milestones, delays, assessments, instructor ratings and notes, attendance and disciplinary records.
  • Identity and compliance documents uploaded by you or your organisation, such as passport and visa details, photographs, medical certificates, licences, regulator reference numbers (for example a DGCA computer number) and signed agreements.
  • Communications: messages, comments, tasks and files shared in the Product, including in discussion rooms.
  • Activity and security data: sign-ins, the actions you take, and an audit log of changes to records (who changed what, when, the values before and after, and the IP address).

Some of these, such as passport details and medical certificates, are sensitive. We restrict them by role and by batch (section 7). Please upload only what your programme needs.

6. Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers process data outside India, for example in the United States. Cadets training at FTOs abroad may also have records created there. Under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, we transfer personal data outside India only to countries the Government of India has not restricted, and only under contracts that require the recipient to protect it.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we process it.
  • Correction and erasure: to correct, complete or update your data, or to have it erased when it is no longer needed.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • Grievance redressal: to complain to us and get a response.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To use these rights, write to our Grievance Officer (section 12). We will verify your identity and reply within the time the law sets, and in any case within 90 days. For data inside a Product, we may pass your request to your organisation, which decides on it as the Data Fiduciary, and help it respond.

10. Children

Under Indian law a child is anyone under 18. Our Products are designed for adult cadets, staff and instructors. If a Customer enrols anyone under 18, the Customer must obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before that person's data is entered, and we will not track, behaviourally monitor or target advertising at them. Our Sites are not directed at children.
